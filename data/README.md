# data/

Tables used by the **Flight Configurator** to compose a loadout station by station. They are copies, byte for byte, of the tables of the Loadout Editor of `dcs-mission-tools` (commit `03834f3ce7858af3aac4e7e0ffbd2f76cceccb3e`), where `data/README.md` describes their shape and how to regenerate them (`aircraft_masses.json` and `loadout_rules.json` are copies from commit `1cdf22afd320e86b69805350a702ce0c4386f88c`).

- `weapon_names.json`, `aircraft_pylons.json`, `aircraft_limits.json`: generated from [pydcs](https://github.com/pydcs/dcs) **1.0.0**, **LGPL-3.0-or-later** ([`COPYING.LESSER`](COPYING.LESSER)). The tables carry the same licence.
- `aircraft_masses.json`: the empty weight and the recommended maximum weight of the four aircraft types the Flight Configurator composes, for its take-off weight estimate (empty weight + fuel + stores; gun and countermeasures not counted). Values read from the aircraft definitions of DCS World (`M_empty`, `M_max`) and checked against the mission editor, **no licence added** (they are values). Byte for byte the same file as in `dcs-mission-tools`.
- `loadout_rules.json`: per aircraft type, the rules between stations that the DCS mission editor applies (`x`: a weapon that does not go with another station's load; `r`: a weapon that needs another one), read from the aircraft definitions of DCS World, **no licence added** (they are facts read from the game; the definitions themselves are not part of this repository). Byte for byte the same file as in `dcs-mission-tools`, where `data/README.md` describes its shape and how to regenerate it. The Flight Configurator reads it with the rules block copied from the Loadout Editor.
- `aircraft_types.json`: the game's flyable aircraft types for **HQ** (a squadron's aircraft in the wing configuration), from pydcs 1.0.0 `dcs/helicopters.py` and `dcs/planes.py` (the classes with `flyable = True`, by id, the type name in a .miz), **LGPL-3.0-or-later**. `aircraft_types_block.py` embeds it in HQ and stops the build unless it holds 12 helicopters and 63 airplanes, without duplicates.
- `aircraft_views.json` and `views/`: the front-view drawings of the Mi-24P, Mi-8MT, F-4E-45MC and Mirage F1EE, by **Goldwolf** (DCS Reference Guide), used with permission. They are **not covered by the repository's AGPL licence**: do not reuse them without the author's permission (see [`views/LICENSE.md`](views/LICENSE.md)).

`build_flight_configurator.py` embeds the tables in the module between `/* NAME:BEGIN */` and `/* NAME:END */` markers, and stops the build if an embedded copy differs from its file.
