Mission Plan v2 is one suite with one version number, opened from a single home page.

**What's new**

- **One way in.** The home page opens every tool and loads the STEPPE WIND example (Example ▾: Mission Commander or Flight Leader). A tool opened on its own points you back to it.
- **One work file.** Save writes the briefing, recon photo, groups, routes, layers, datacards, theatre and settings into one `.json`; Open brings it all back, with Restore for a few seconds.
- **The Mission Commander → Flight Leader → Mission Commander circuit.** The Mission Commander prepares the mission (HQ, Briefing Generator, Recon Station); the Flight Leader prepares the flights (Route Planner, Datacard Generator and the new **Flight Configurator**: identity, radios, loadout on the aircraft's front view, livery); HQ applies the flight packages to the mission.
- **Groups shared** by the Flight Configurator, Route Planner and Datacard Generator.
- **One top bar and the same words in every tool**, sized for touch, readable in every theme.

The full list, tool by tool, is in the [CHANGELOG](https://github.com/MirabelleBenou/dcs-mission-plan/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md).

**Download**

`dcs_mission_plan.html` — the whole suite in one file: open it in a browser, no install. Everything works offline except the Route Planner's map backgrounds and airfield chart sheets, which load online. Or use it online: https://mirabellebenou.github.io/dcs-mission-plan/
